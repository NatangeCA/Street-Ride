FTP ramp test

    

      

      
  • Begin pedaling at a comfortable cadence.
  • Your trainer will maintain watts by adjusting resistance.
  • The test will end when your cadence drops out.

      
How your FTP is calculated: after a 1-minute warm-up, the trainer raises your power one step every minute. Your best 1-minute power is multiplied by 0.75 to estimate your FTP. Press Skip step if a step feels too easy.

      
Demo mode: a rider with no trainer connected gets a simulated rider. Nothing from a demo is saved to your history.
